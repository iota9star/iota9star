## 👨‍💻 About Me

Hey, I'm **iota9star** — a full-stack engineer who has been turning coffee into commits since August 2016 ☕📅, currently at **102 public repos** and showing no signs of stopping.
My home turf is the **Rust ↔ Flutter boundary** 🦀📱: FFI bridges, rendering-adjacent widgets, and the kind of glue code that has to be *both* fast and boring.
Server-side I reach for **Axum** and **Spring** ☕ when p99 latency and horizontal scale actually matter — microservices tuned by measurement, not vibes. 📏
Client-side I ship with **Flutter**, **React** ⚛️, and **Vue** 💚, because a feature isn't finished until it feels good in someone's hand.
I work **with AI agents in the loop** 🤖 every day — they draft, I architect, and the quality gates stay firmly on. ✅
Off the clock: seasonal anime 🌸, BTC / EVM / IC rabbit holes ⛓️, and benchmarking things nobody asked me to benchmark. 🔬
