## 👨‍💻 About Me

My handle is a growth story in two syllables. 🌱 **Iota** is the smallest letter the Greek alphabet has to offer; **star** is what that smallest thing becomes when you keep showing up for it. Every project I've shipped started as an iota — one missing helper, one awkward API, one app that should have existed and didn't — and earned its asterisk the honest way: iteration. 🔁

By day I build the machinery: **Rust and Java** services with Axum and Spring, microservices engineered to fail loudly in logs and never in front of users. 🏗️ But I've never bought the dividing line between backend and frontend, because software is ultimately judged by a thumb tapping a screen. So I stay fluent across **Flutter, React, Vue, Android, and TV** — a great p99 is worthless if the button it serves feels dead. ⚡

My open-source home is [fluttercandies](https://github.com/fluttercandies), where I maintain a shelf of small, type-safe, dependency-lean Dart packages: routing, date-time math, concurrency limits, functional error handling, codegen. 🧩 The quiet infrastructure nobody notices — until it isn't there. Together with my own apps, they've gathered **1.7k+ ⭐** from people I'll never meet, and it still surprises me every time. 🎁

## 🧭 How I Build

- 🎯 **Product first** — software exists so a person can finish something they cared about; everything else is plumbing.
- 🪨 **Push invariants into types** — human memory is fallible; the compiler is not.
- ⚡ **Latency is a feeling** — users can't read your p99, but they can feel your 100 ms.
- 🧹 **Boring on purpose** — the best dependency is the one you forget you installed.
- 🔁 **Small batches, real users** — the feedback loop *is* the feature.
- 🔓 **Default to open** — strangers find the bugs you never would.
