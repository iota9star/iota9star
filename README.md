## 👨‍💻 About Me

Hi there 👋 I'm **iota9star**, a full-stack engineer who treats every layer like it's the *most important* layer — because on any given day, one of them actually is. **Rust** 🦀 when performance is the product, **Java & Kotlin** ☕ when systems must scale without drama, **Dart & Flutter** 📱 when one codebase has to shine on phones, desktops and TVs, and **TypeScript** 🎭 when the browser is the stage.

By day I ship microservices, cross-platform apps, and AI woven into real workflows 🤖 — a genuine quality multiplier, never a party trick. After hours I tend an open-source garden with the [FlutterCandies](https://github.com/fluttercandies) community 🍬: a shelf of published Dart/Flutter packages, a fine-grained signals experiment, and a trio of anime clients that rule my contribution graph 🌸.

🔬 **Currently obsessed with:** Rust-powered JavaScript engines, fine-grained signal reactivity, TV-first D-pad navigation, and convincing the compiler to do my debugging for me 🧠.
