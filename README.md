## 👨‍💻 About Me

Hi, I'm **iota9star** — a full-stack engineer who refuses to pick a lane. 🛤️
I build high-performance services with **Rust** 🦀 and **Java** ☕, then wrap them in frontends with **Flutter** 📱, **React**, and **Vue** that feel as fast as the backends behind them.
I believe product sense is an engineering skill, not a department — the best code is the code that ships the *right* thing. 🎯
Lately I'm fusing **Rust into the Flutter ecosystem** ⚡, maintaining packages with the **fluttercandies** org 🍬, and wiring **AI agents** 🤖 into every step of my workflow.
When I'm off the clock: seasonal anime tracking 🎬, Bitcoin and EVM rabbit holes ⛓️, and the occasional rewrite of something that already worked — just faster.
